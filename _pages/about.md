# 🤖 Selected Projects

## ZYNQ-Based Gesture-Recognition Dexterous Hand
*National Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program · Project Lead · 2025.09 – Present*

- Built a gesture-recognition and dexterous-hand control system around a ZYNQ7020 board and an OV5640 camera.
- Implemented video capture, RGB-to-YCbCr conversion, median filtering, skin-color detection, morphological processing, connected-component analysis, Sobel edge detection, and UDP transmission in the PL pipeline.
- Used static-gesture features to control an 8-DoF, four-finger dexterous hand through Arduino.

## Homebot: An Embodied Dual-Arm Household Humanoid Robot
*Project Lead · 2025.12 – Present*

- Designed a home-service robot with a Raspberry Pi 5 controller, omni-directional Mecanum-wheel base, 60 cm lifting mechanism, dual 6-DoF gripper arms, and five camera streams.
- Used LeRobot for master–slave teleoperation and data collection, and explored π0.5 vision-language-action models for household task execution.
